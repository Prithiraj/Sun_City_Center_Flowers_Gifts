# Sun City Center Flowers & Gifts

Responsive, photo-led static storefront for Sun City Center Flowers & Gifts in Sun City Center, Florida.

## Live site

https://prithiraj.github.io/Sun_City_Center_Flowers_Gifts/

## Design direction

The site blends three supplied reference directions without copying their invented business content:

- local-florist warmth and immediate call / directions actions,
- modern ecommerce category browsing,
- editorial floral-studio typography, cream paper tones, and asymmetrical photography.

Business identity, address, phone details, and linked categories are based on the florist’s public ordering site. Dynamic facts such as current hours, prices, delivery cut-offs, and product availability are intentionally left to the live ordering site or confirmed by phone.

## Tech

- Semantic HTML5
- Responsive CSS
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Real floral photography from Unsplash
- No build step
- GitHub Pages compatible
